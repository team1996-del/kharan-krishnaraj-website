ABOUT

Kharan Krishnaraj

Brand Strategist working with founder-led businesses at the point where growth begins exposing weaknesses in how the brand is understood, expressed and managed.

BELIEF

A brand should be understood before it is designed.

My work grew from seeing the same problem repeatedly.

Businesses were investing in identities, websites, content and campaigns before becoming clear about what the brand was actually meant to stand for.

The visible work changed. The underlying confusion often remained.

That led me toward a different model: understand the business first, define the strategic logic behind the brand, then build the expression and systems around it.

WHERE I WORK

Between founder intent and brand execution.

I help leadership clarify the logic behind the brand, then work with the people responsible for translating that logic into identity, messaging, content and customer-facing expression.
